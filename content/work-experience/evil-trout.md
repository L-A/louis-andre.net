With Robin Ward, of Evil Trout inc., I created websites for [The Roottrees are Dead](https://roottrees.com) and [The Incident At Galley House](https://galley.house), two thinky games and game jam darlings, each of which got re-made for a Steam release (to great success!). I also ran a round of style and structure updates to the [Evil Trout website](https://eviltrout.com) itself. I’m particularly proud of the accessible curly ribbon navigation in that one.

I was responsible for both the design and development of the websites, which allowed the nimble team to stay focused on their own development tasks.
