Well, first there's this very website! I've updated it, in large part because I had picked a tech stack that turned into a hamster wheel made of package updates and deprecation warnings. I just want a place where I can write and make fun web things.

I keep thinking about [Malleable software](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/), and I think I'll be thinking about it for the foreseeable future.

As I write this, I'm having my first _real_ lull in client projects since 2019-2020. I don't know what my next project will be. I remember feeling somewhat jealous during the Covid lockdown, because most of the people around me had to adapt to suddenly getting more free time, while I got swamped with work and prospects. The inertia held until 2026.

I've been very slow at resuming my writing and generative art practice lately. I feel I was taken out of the game by parenthood and a rough year of managing chronic anxiety (both happening at the same time, of course). I've made time for a few scattershot articles in 2025, which feels pretty good – I noticed the uptick when laying out the new [Journal](/journal) page on this site.
